A Brief History

From Humble Sail-Making Roots to Global Tensile Mastery

From humble sail-making beginnings, Structurflex began life as Sails & Covers Ltd on 21 July 1937. Across more than eight decades, Structurflex has consistently pursued innovation, developing proprietary fabrication techniques to become one of the world's leading suppliers of innovative tensile membrane structures.

In 2005, Structurflex Middle East Contracting LLC was established in Dubai, United Arab Emirates as regional representative operations for the Structurflex Group to pursue the rapidly expanding demand in the MENA region for complex tensioned membrane architecture.

In 2012, management responsibility of Structurflex Middle East Contracting LLC moved to Mr. Maged Mounir, ushering in a transformative era of regional expansion, federal contracting awards, and prestigious international commissions.
