Data Governance & Confidentiality

Privacy Policy & Technical Data Protection

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Structurflex Middle East Contracting LLC (SFXMEC)

1. Scope and Technical Data Governance

Structurflex Middle East Contracting LLC (“SFXMEC”, “we”, “us”) recognizes the strategic and commercial value of engineering documents, BIM models, finite element analysis (FEA) files, shop drawings, and proprietary architectural data entrusted to us by project developers, architects, and main contractors.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information provided directly through project tender submissions, consultation requests, and contract executions, including architectural drawing sets, engineering load parameters, commercial billing coordinates, and communication records.

3. Non-Disclosure & Commercial Confidentiality

All architectural plans, CAD/Revit models, and structural engineering studies submitted through our platform or direct tender inquiries are treated as strictly confidential commercial assets. We do not sell, rent, or distribute client project documents to third parties.

4. Contact Data Protection Officer

For inquiries regarding our technical data governance or to request non-disclosure agreement (NDA) execution prior to tender document sharing, contact our Dubai headquarters at:
