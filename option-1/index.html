Turnkey Workflow

OUR CAPABILITIES

From initial mathematical form-finding to lifetime structural stewardship, Structurflex Middle East manages the entire project lifecycle in-house.

Step 01

Design

Parametric form-finding, 3D architectural geometry, conceptual equilibrium modeling & digital prototyping.

Step 02

Engineering

Advanced membrane analysis, non-linear FEA, computational wind load modeling, and structural cable sizing.

Step 03

Fabrication

State-of-the-art cleanroom manufacturing, automated CNC precision cutting, and high-frequency molecular seaming.

Step 04

Installation

Expert site execution, certified master rigging, crane erection, laser-measured pre-stressing, and stringent HSE.

Step 05

Inspection & AMC

Regular membrane condition audits, non-destructive cleaning, cable re-tensioning, and lifetime preventive maintenance.
