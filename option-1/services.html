Comprehensive Structural Portfolio

Engineered Solutions for Every Structural Need

From high-performance architectural tensile membranes to air-supported inflatable domes and lifetime AMC stewardship across the Middle East and Africa.

Solution 01

Architectural Structures (ETFE & PTFE)

We engineer iconic translucent ETFE skylights, pneumatic cushions, and PTFE architectural envelopes. ETFE transmits over 95% of natural light while maintaining high thermal insulation (U-values of 1.6 - 2.0 W/m²K in multi-layer cushions) and self-cleaning non-stick surfaces.

  • Single Skin and Multi-Layer Pneumatic ETFE Cushions
  • Non-combustible PTFE Woven Fiberglass Roofing (DIN 4102 A2)
  • Parametric Atriums, Skylights, and High-Performance Facades

Solution 02

Sports & Recreation Structures

Engineered specifically for extreme Middle Eastern heat, our column-free sports roofs cover entire school campus courts, Olympic athletic tracks, tennis courts, and football stadium grandstands.

  • Wide-span Arched Tubular Steel Trusses without interior columns
  • 75%–85% Solar Heat Deflection keeping outdoor courts cool
  • Tested for severe wind loadings compliant with GCC building codes

Solution 04

Transport & Infrastructure Structures

Aerodynamic, heavy-duty tensile canopies engineered for high pedestrian volume transport hubs: RTA marine floating stations, airport passenger arrival concourses, rail hubs, and metro footbridges.

  • High resistance to coastal saline moisture, sand, and humidity
  • Aerodynamic uplift engineering compliant with international airport standards
  • Self-cleaning titanium dioxide (TiO2) coatings for zero dust adherence

Solution 05

Industrial & Warehouse Structures

Clear-span industrial bulk storage covers, logistics transfer shelters, and agricultural environmental enclosures. Designed to protect sensitive industrial assets from harsh GCC ambient conditions with minimal foundation requirements.

  • High-volume clear height for industrial cranes and heavy machinery
  • Tear-resistant multi-layer polyester PVC with flame retardancy (NFPA 701)
  • Fast turnkey execution at a fraction of the cost of heavy steel sheds

Air-Supported Structures (ASATI)

In strategic partnership with ASATI (Air Structures American Technologies, Inc.), Structurflex ME delivers cutting-edge air-supported dome structures. Offering unprecedented unobstructed clear spans up to 150m+, ideal for multisport arenas, seasonal exhibition halls, and climate-controlled storage.

  • 100% column-free interior volume supported by continuous air pressure
  • Smart automated inflation and HVAC thermal management systems
  • Rapid deployment and demountability for temporary or permanent use

Solution 07

Lifecycle Services (Inspection & AMC)

Ensuring tension membrane structures retain structural safety, aesthetic brilliance, and weather tightness for 35+ years. Our certified rope-access engineers provide comprehensive inspections, tension calibrations, ultrasonic seam audits, and regular maintenance contracts.

  • Laser measurement of boundary cable pre-tensioning and deflection
  • Ultrasonic weld inspection and seam tear strength testing
  • Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) with guaranteed emergency response
