Comprehensive Structural Portfolio

Engineered Solutions for Every Structural Need

From high-performance architectural tensile membranes to pneumatic ETFE daylighting and ASATI air-supported inflatable sports domes across the GCC.

01

Architectural Tensile Structures

Custom-engineered PTFE and PVDF architectural membrane canopies, curved hyperbolic paraboloids, and conic structures engineered for harsh desert UV resistance and high wind loads.

02

ETFE Foil & Cushion Atriums

Pneumatic 2 to 4-layer ETFE cushions providing maximum natural daylighting, self-cleaning non-stick surfaces, and up to 95% reduction in substructure steel tonnage compared to glass.

03

Air-Supported Sports Domes

Exclusive Middle East & Africa partner for ASATI. Massive column-free structures spanning football pitches, Olympic pools, and tennis centers with integrated automated HVAC systems.

04

Sports & Stadium Canopies

Monumental cantilevered grandstand roofs, velodromes, and horse racing canopies engineered to FIFA tournament codes with aerodynamic deflection and thermal shading.

05

Tensile Facades & Cable Mesh

Three-dimensional textile building envelopes and stainless steel cable netting that dramatically modernize building aesthetics while reducing internal solar heat loads by up to 70%.

06

Lifecycle Inspection & AMC

Turnkey preventative asset stewardship, ultrasonic tension recalibrations, specialized membrane pressure washing, and 24/7 emergency response across the UAE and GCC.
