Company Narrative

From Maritime Craftsmanship to Iconic Landmarks

Founded in 1937, Structurflex began with maritime sailmaking craftsmanship in New Zealand, mastering the manipulation of high-tension textiles under harsh oceanic wind loads. Over eight decades, this expertise evolved into a world-leading architectural tensile contracting practice.

Today, Structurflex Middle East Contracting LLC (SFXMEC) delivers complete turnkey engineering, computer form-finding, specialized fabrication, and certified rope-access erection across the United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and wider Africa.
