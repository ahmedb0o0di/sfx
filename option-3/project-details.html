Abu Dhabi Shooting Club PTFE Roof

A 6,200 sq.m landmark architectural PTFE fiberglass tensile membrane roof featuring high-tension flying masts, engineered to endure harsh desert coastal winds and thermal extremes.

Case Study Narrative

Architectural Intent & Project Overview

The Abu Dhabi Shooting Club required an iconic, sun-shading architectural envelope that could protect spectators and athletes from the extreme Arabian Gulf solar radiance while maintaining natural air circulation and daytime illumination.

Structurflex Middle East Contracting LLC was commissioned as the specialist design-build contractor to engineer, fabricate, and erect a sweeping tensile membrane structure spanning over 6,200 square meters. The client’s vision mandated an aerodynamic, sweeping silhouette inspired by traditional bedouin tents, reimagined through 21st-century aerospace tension engineering.

Engineering Challenges & Technical Solutions

Structural Challenge

High gusting desert winds coupled with extreme ambient thermal expansion (reaching up to 55°C) created severe biaxial strain variations across the membrane panels, risking fabric ponding and stress fatigue.

Engineered Solution

Implemented non-linear geometric finite element analysis (FEA) to calculate optimal biaxial compensation ratios. Integrated floating tension masts with stainless steel prestressed stay cables to absorb dynamic wind buffeting.

FEA Form-Finding & Material Selection

Tensile membranes require form-finding computation rather than conventional static load analysis. Using specialized multi-body non-linear algorithms, Structurflex structural engineers modeled the membrane boundary geometries to guarantee zero wrinkling and maintain continuous positive curvature under all combined dead, live, and aerodynamic uplift conditions.

Material Specification: Premium PTFE Coated Fiberglass

  • ✓ Class A Fire Rating: 100% non-combustible conforming to ASTM E136 and UAE Civil Defense codes.
  • ✓ Self-Cleaning Fluoropolymer Coating: Repels desert sand, oil mist, and atmospheric particulate through rainwater rinse.
  • ✓ Solar Reflectance Index (SRI) > 80%: Blocks 99% of damaging UV while transmitting 14% soft diffused natural daylight.
  • ✓ Design Working Life: 30+ years without micro-cracking or polymer embrittlement.

Precision Rigging & On-Site Erection

The erection sequencing required synchronized hydraulic jacks tensioning opposing boundary cables in pre-calculated increments of 5 kilonewtons. This calibrated jacking sequence ensured uniform membrane stress distribution, preserving the structural warranty and achieving millimeter-level precision across all mast anchor foundations.
