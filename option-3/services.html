Comprehensive Structural Solutions

Specialized Tensile Architectural Capabilities

From high-performance architectural tensile membranes to pneumatic ETFE daylighting and ASATI air-supported inflatable sports domes across the GCC.

Architectural Tensile Structures

Custom-engineered PTFE and PVDF membrane roofs, conical canopies, and hyperbolic forms engineered for extreme desert wind resilience and UV endurance.

ETFE Foil & Cushion Atriums

Pneumatic 2 to 4-layer ETFE cushions providing maximum natural daylighting, self-cleaning surfaces, and dramatic reduction in steel tonnage compared to glass.

Air-Supported Sports Domes

Exclusive Middle East & Africa partner for ASATI. Massive column-free structures spanning sports halls, football venues, and swimming complexes.

Lifecycle Inspection & AMC

Ultrasonic tension testing, specialized cleaning, structural seam integrity audits, and 24/7 emergency response across the UAE and GCC.
