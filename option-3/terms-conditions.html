Commercial & Engineering Terms of Service

Contractual standards, ASTM & DIN design parameters, and warranty protocols governing turnkey structural contracting.

1. Engineering Calculations & Stamped Deliverables

All non-linear finite element analysis (FEA) models, boundary reaction schedules, and connection details provided during preliminary tender phases remain the intellectual property of Structurflex Middle East Contracting LLC until formal contract execution.

2. Design Standards & Compliance

Turnkey structures executed by SFXMEC are calculated in strict compliance with ASCE 55-16 (“Tensile Membrane Structures”), Dubai Municipality Building Codes, ASTM E108 flame-spread standards, and DIN 4102 fire ratings.

3. Warranty Conditions

Material and workmanship warranties ranging from 10 to 25 years are subject to adherence to Structurflex Operations & Maintenance (O&M) manuals and certified annual tension and seam audits.
